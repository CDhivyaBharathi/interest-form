Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This site lets Ashoka University students register interest in volunteering at the Makerspace (Mphasis AI & Applied Tech Lab, Ashoka University). This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Full name
  • Ashoka email ID
  • Undergraduate batch
  • The interest pools you select, and anything you choose to write in “What would you like to learn?”

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email address from your Google account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts, or any other Google data.

How we use it

Only to understand interest in Makerspace volunteering and to contact you if there is a next step. Submitting the form does not confirm you as a volunteer.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Responses are stored in a Google Sheet managed by the Makerspace team, via Google Apps Script. Only the Makerspace team can view them. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties, except Google as our storage provider. This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies.

Retention and your choices

We keep responses while volunteer recruitment is active. To see, correct, or delete your data, email makerspace@ashoka.edu.in.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Contact
